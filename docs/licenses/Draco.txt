Draco 3D compression decoder

The browser decoder supplied with Three.js is distributed under the Apache
License, Version 2.0. See Apache-2.0.txt in this directory.

Source: https://github.com/google/draco
Three.js decoder distribution: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/jsm/libs/draco

The decoder binaries are bundled unchanged by Vite. Geometry is encoded during
asset preparation using draco3dgltf and decoded locally in browser Web Workers.
